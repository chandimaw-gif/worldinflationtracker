Methodology

Which figures are official?

The headline and core inflation figures on the Dashboard are official: they are copied from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka's monthly CCPI releases, which use Department of Census and Statistics data. We do not calculate them. The chart starts in December 2022, because the Central Bank's earlier tables use a different base year (2013=100).

Our own price index: not yet published

We are building an independent index from the prices in our Price Log, using the method described below. It is not published yet, because we do not yet have enough consistent price history across enough items for it to be reliable. We will publish it once it has at least 12 months of regularly collected prices, and will state any limits it has at that time.

1. Planned Method for Our Index — Laspeyres Fixed-Basket CPI

Our index is designed to use the modified Laspeyres index, consistent with the methodology used by Sri Lanka's Department of Census and Statistics (CCPI base 2021=100) and the ILO/IMF Consumer Price Index Manual (2020).

CPI Formula

CPIt = (Σ (Pi,t × Qi,0)) / (Σ (Pi,0 × Qi,0)) × 100

  • Pi,t = Price of item i in period t (current)
  • Pi,0 = Price of item i in the base period
  • Qi,0 = Quantity (fixed weight) of item i in the base period

Inflation Rate Calculations

Year-on-Year (YoY): πt = ((CPIt − CPIt−12) / CPIt−12) × 100

Month-on-Month (MoM): πm = ((CPIt − CPIt−1) / CPIt−1) × 100

12-Month Moving Average: πMA = ((Avg CPI last 12 mo − Avg CPI previous 12 mo) / Avg CPI previous 12 mo) × 100

Core Inflation (Exclusion Method)

Following the CBSL paper "Revisiting Core Inflation in Sri Lanka" (Maheepala & Pathirage, December 2024), we exclude:

  • Volatile food items (fresh vegetables, fruits, coconut, eggs, chillies, onions)
  • Energy (electricity, gas, liquid fuels, solid fuels)
  • Transport fuels
  • Water supply

2. Elementary Index Compilation

Following DCS methodology, elementary indices are designed to use the geometric mean (Jevons formula):

PJevons = (Π Pi)(1/n)

Higher-level indices are designed to use the Laspeyres formula with expenditure weights from HIES 2019.

3. Data Sources and Update Frequency

  • Official CCPI: Central Bank of Sri Lanka monthly press releases (Department of Census and Statistics data). Added after each release.
  • Fuel prices: CEYPETCO website, checked daily.
  • Retail prices: Spar 2U listings, checked daily, plus manually entered prices. Keells is planned but is not yet collected.
  • USD/LKR: CBSL TT selling rate, checked three times a day, from June 2026. Earlier history comes from x-rates.com and open.er-api.com, and each row shows its source.
  • Bank exchange rates: rates published by individual banks, collected daily. A row is hidden if its selling rate is below its buying rate.
  • US 10-year yield: US Treasury, daily.
  • News: daily list of headlines, linked to the original publishers.

4. Important Disclaimers

Disclaimer: World Inflation Tracker is an independent research tool. Apart from figures labelled "Official", anything published here is NOT an official government statistic. For official inflation figures, please refer to the Department of Census and Statistics and the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

Data Attribution: Product prices are sourced from publicly accessible e-commerce websites. News headlines link to their original publishers. Each exchange rate shows its source.
