About World Inflation Tracker

World Inflation Tracker brings Sri Lanka's official inflation figures, logged consumer prices and exchange rates together in one place, with the source and date shown for each. It starts with Sri Lanka, and is built so that other countries can be added later.

Data sources and update frequency

  • Official inflation (CCPI, 2021=100): Central Bank of Sri Lanka press releases, using Department of Census and Statistics data. Monthly, after each release.
  • USD/LKR: CBSL TT selling rate, checked three times a day. Before June 2026 the history uses other sources, labelled on the Exchange Rates page.
  • Bank exchange rates: rates published by individual banks. Daily.
  • Consumer prices: CEYPETCO fuel prices and Spar 2U listings, checked daily, plus some manually entered prices. See the Price Log.
  • News links: a list of headlines that link to the original publishers. Daily.

More detail is on the Methodology page.

Limitations

  • Online prices may not reflect market prices, particularly for fresh produce.
  • Our price log covers far fewer items than the official index.
  • Our own price index is still in development and has not been published. See the Methodology page.

Corrections

If you find an error in our data, please contact us with the page, date and figure. We will check it against the source and correct it.
